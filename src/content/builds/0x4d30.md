## Goal
A small 4-axis arm that can pick and place blocks, controlled by a Raspberry Pi.

## Progress
The parts have been machined. Next is wiring the components together and writing the inverse-kinematics loops.

> "What I cannot create, I do not understand."

## Parts

- Aluminium extrusion frame
- 4x MG996R servos
- Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W
- Custom PCB for power distribution
