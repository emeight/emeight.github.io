### September 7th, 2026
The first attempt.

> This was a pretty rough first pass, logging for learning purposes.

I combined the recipes from [Skandinavisches Roggenbrot](https://cookidoo.com.cn/recipes/recipe/en-CN/r419171) and [German Rye Without Sourdough](https://humbly-homemade.com/roggenbrot/) while taking some unwitting liberties. 

#### Recipe
Mixed these together then let sit for 20 minutes.
- Water, 350g
- Sugar, 30g
- Honey, 2 tbsp
- Instant Dry Yeast, 2 tbsp
- Dark Rye Flour, 300g

Combined the above with the following after it had sat.
- Salt, 2 tbsp
- White Flour, 100g 
- Buttermilk, 220g
- Weihenstephaner Hefe Weissbier, half bottle

Topped with these two, then let it rise for an hour.
- Sesame seeds
- Black sesame seeds

Baked at 400°F for 35 minutes then let it sit on the counter top in the pan for another 30 minutes.

#### Result
I knew it was going to be rough because the dough was too wet to even knead. Basically a malformed banana bread that tasted a little sour.

![rye_0001_slice](/assets/images/bread/rye_0001_slice.png)

#### Lookahead
The dough was way too wet, need to cut back on the liquids (probably no beer next time).
